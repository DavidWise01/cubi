# CUBI 159 — Toroidal Geometry-to-Orbital Correspondence Test

User geometry: two front/back × two upper/lower × two left/right × three local positions = 24 unique toroidal addresses around `{inf - + inf} 0`.

Null correspondence hypothesis H0: **one toroidal address equals one unique electron quantum state**. For neutral Z-electron atoms, the Pauli principle requires Z distinct occupied complete one-electron states. Therefore H0 has capacity 24; it fails for all neutral elements Z=25..118, including Og with a 94-state shortfall. It is not evidence against every toroidal model: extra internal quantum degrees of freedom, times, multiple linked toroids or additional orbitals could increase state capacity, but must be precisely specified and tested without assigning target labels after the fact.

Crucial distinction: 24! permutations cannot turn 24 simultaneous single-occupancy addresses into 24! simultaneous available electron states. Temporal traversal also does not by itself account for electron antisymmetry and simultaneous multielectron spectra. This is a falsification of a *specific naive one-to-one mapping*, not a measurement of particle shape or a refutation of all possible field topologies.

JavaScript `mapping159.js` implements the transparent capacity calculation and displays failures at Z=25 and Z=118. Theoretical baseline only, not experimental ab initio computation. No new Lean. Browser runtime unverified.
