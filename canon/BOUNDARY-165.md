# CUBI 165 — Relativistically Bounded Classical Circulation

Set cell side `a=2×1.61658e-35 m`, radii `R=r*a`, `r∈{9,6,3,1}`, and `c=299792458 m/s`. For a classical circular streamline, `v=Γ/(2πR)`. Apply the constraint `|Γ|≤2πRc` at every band. The smallest radius r=1 defines the most restrictive shared bound; choosing Γ=f×2πac for 0≤f≤1 yields v/c=f/r and passes all four speed checks, preserving identical Γ by construction. At demonstration f=0.5, the speed fractions at 9,6,3,1 are 1/18,1/12,1/6,1/2.

Independent band-normalized Γ_r=f×2πR_rc also passes the speed limits, but Γ_r varies with radius and therefore is **not conserved as one shared circulation value**. In either interpretation the toroidal model has not derived the electron's intrinsic spin, mass, charge, spectra or physical structure. The bound is meaningful for classical fluid motion only; field phase velocities need not represent particle velocities.
