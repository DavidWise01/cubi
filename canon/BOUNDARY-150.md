# CUBI 150 — Action-to-Reaction Persistence Boundary

Substrate `{{aether}}`; geometry `{ [6x4(5x3)] }`.

An authorized symbolic action can be observed and reported as a separate reaction record. Action, reaction, and comparison retain independent types and their own causal provenance. LH, CH, and RH do not surrender separate identities or vote. No physical effect is inferred from a symbolic database record.

The Lean 4 source `proofs/CUBI150.lean` models append-only history, length continuity, unchanged reload, and preservation of appended event identity. These are theorems about an abstract pure-list specification, not proof of IndexedDB durability, asynchronous writes, crash recovery, or the running browser implementation.

No Lean compiler was available to execute the proof in this environment. The attempted JavaScript implementation was blocked before commit; do not mark the runtime boundary deployed. No consensus or quorum.
