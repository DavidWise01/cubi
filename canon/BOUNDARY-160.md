# CUBI 160 — Four Cardinal Paired-Flow Units Raised to Four

User notation: `{{ -+ n , -+s , -+e , -+w }}^4`.

Literal software interpretation: each of N, S, E and W bears one inseparable `-+` flow pair, and this ordered four-direction motif is repeated four times. Therefore there are 4 × 4 = **16 paired units** and 32 polarity endpoints, **not** 32 electrons or necessarily 32 independent quantum states. Each pair has an identity and ring index; the resulting symbolic traversal is closed under modulo-16 successor. An interactive test is in `directions160.js`.

This new 16-unit directional motif cannot be equated to the 24-address structure without an explicit mapping; they remain separately defined. Likewise, 4th power here means repetition, not automatically exponentiation of the number of accessible microstates.
