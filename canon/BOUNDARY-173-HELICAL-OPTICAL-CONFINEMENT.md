# CUBI 173 — Helical transport and optical confinement hypothesis

User-proposed mechanism: light enters or circulates through an Archimedes-screw-like helical structure; Bernoulli-type flow is invoked as a mechanical analogy; toroidal circulation is hypothesized to confine light, producing stationary-looking projection and repeated intersections of ONE underlying -+ state.

Scientific separation:
1. Archimedes screw = helical transport geometry; not by itself an optical trap.
2. Bernoulli law p + rho*v²/2 + rho*g*z = constant is conditional on stationary, inviscid, incompressible flow along a streamline; not a vacuum photon equation.
3. Light confinement requires specified electromagnetic boundary conditions, refractive index contrast, waveguide modes, total internal reflection, or cavity resonances, plus loss budget. No such confinement demonstrated yet in this hypothesis.
4. If optical phase velocity, group velocity, and signal velocity differ, report separately; do not fit parameters solely to force v<=c. Any claim of information travelling faster than c requires a causal test and independent experimental verification.
5. Observation: projected motion may look stationary if repetitive dynamics alias with sampling, without proving electron structure or photon trapping.
