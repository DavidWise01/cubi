# CUBI 168 — Coupled Energy Transfer Dynamics

The CUBI167 energy reservoir was defined residually. CUBI168 now specifies a candidate dynamical transfer law: `dK/dt = k_in R - k_out K` and `dR/dt = -k_in R + k_out K`, for nonnegative rates and nonnegative initial compartment energies. Adding the two differential equations yields `d(K+R)/dt=0` independently of state, rather than redefining the reservoir after the fact. With positive rates the equilibrium `K*=E*k_in/(k_in+k_out)` is attractive and nonnegative. This law relaxes to equilibrium; it does NOT create a 9→1→9 oscillator by itself.

Implementation: `dynamics168.js`, integrated into GitHub Pages. It runs 1000 explicit Euler steps with dt=.005 and checks energy drift, positivity and approach to equilibrium. At rate sum <=4, dt*(rate sum)<=.02, so the discretization is a positive convex transfer and conserves the total up to floating-point error. The initial energies are K=.0015, R=.2, with default rates k_in=.8, k_out=.2. The coupled law is a phenomenological two-reservoir model; it does not derive electron field dynamics, quantum spin, or a magnetic-fluid reservoir from experiment. Browser execution not independently verified. No new Lean files.
