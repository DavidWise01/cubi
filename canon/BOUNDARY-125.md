# CUBI 125 — Large Rectangular Primitive Field

Canonical expression: `{ [6x4(5x3)] }`.

The **BRACE** encloses a large rectangular field; inside are 24 distinct BOX data addresses arranged 6 columns × 4 rows and 15 distinct TOROID tool addresses arranged 5 columns × 3 rows. The parenthesized tool region remains type-isolated even though it appears nested inside the bracketed field. The earlier primitive powers `[]^4`, `()^3`, and `{n}^{n}` retain their symbolic identities; 6×4 and 5×3 are explicit layout counts, not replacements for primitive exponents.

A Canvas2D visualization draws the entire rectangle, its 24 boxes, 15 tool ellipses, and enclosing scope. Audit isolation reports counts and automatic-execution=false. Bind sample explicitly demonstrates a non-mutating, derived symbolic record; no tool is executable by containment alone. No checkpoints, quorums or votes.

**Boundary:** this release's explicit-binding sample is in-memory and does not append its derived result to persistent IndexedDB. Browser render has not been independently verified. This layout is a software primitive arrangement, not a measured physical lattice.
