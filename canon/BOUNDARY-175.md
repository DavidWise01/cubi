# CUBI 175 — Two-manifold punctuation boundary

User specification: `..||..|...||, || ,||...|..||..`. Ignore commas/whitespace as separators. Left block has 12 symbols (7 dots, 5 bars); right block is the exact character reversal of left; center has 2 bars. Full sequence is a 26-symbol palindrome with 14 dots and 12 bars. This is verified combinatorial symmetry, not yet a physical interface.

`interface175.js` computes reflection and transmission from an explicit *assumed* 1D, lossless, normal-incidence linear wave model. Dot cells have index 1 and bar cells index 1.5 (arbitrary demonstration), common unit geometric thickness, ambient and exit index 1. A characteristic-matrix product with layer phase 2*pi*n/lambda yields intensity reflectance R and transmittance T. With real positive indices and no absorption, R+T=1 to numerical precision. Mirror symmetry alone does NOT guarantee R=0, T=1, trapping, faster-than-light transport, or two physical manifold regimes. Frequency-dependent resonances can be tested by changing wavelength.

Proposed gravity-push/void-pull manifold interpretations remain hypotheses. The current model lacks a gravitational metric, causal cone, particle worldline, phase/group-delay or stored-energy dynamics. No Lean files; browser execution unverified.
